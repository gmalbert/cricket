# Wicket Oracle — 6-Month Feature Roadmap

## Month 1: Live Match Experience

- **Live scorecard widget** — Refresh CricketData.org live feed every 60 seconds during a match.
- **Current run rate vs. required** — For chasing teams, show CRR / RRR and projected final score.
- **Real-time win probability** — Update win probability bar as wickets and runs change.
- **Match commentary feed** — Stream recent ball-by-ball commentary (last 5 deliveries).

## Month 2: Player Spotlight

- **Player prop calculator** — Surface DraftKings player run-scoring and wicket props with model comparison.
- **Top performers today** — Leaderboard of current-match batting/bowling leaders.
- **Fantasy points projection** — Estimated DraftKings fantasy points for the top 10 players in today's games.

## Month 3: Historical Analytics

- **H2H team history** — Last 10 IPL meetings between today's opponents.
- **Venue scoring history** — Average first/second innings scores at each ground for the last 3 seasons.
- **Purple/Orange cap race** — Running leaderboard for season's top run scorer and wicket taker.

## Month 4: Advanced Analytics

- **Impact player analysis** — Who performs above expectation vs. below at specific venues.
- **Powerplay analysis** — Team and player powerplay stats with comparisons.
- **Death overs specialist tracker** — Which bowlers are most economical in overs 17–20.

## Month 5: Betting Intelligence

- **Value bets page** — Edge > 3% picks with DraftKings lines and no-vig implied probability.
- **Historical betting ROI** — Backtest model picks against past IPL seasons.
- **Live line movement** — Show how DraftKings shifts odds as the match evolves.

## Month 6: Automation & Notifications

- **Pre-match email at noon** — Today's fixtures with top picks and key matchup context.
- **Wicket alert** — Discord/email notification when a key player is dismissed in a live game.
- **Season summary report** — Auto-generated PDF/HTML at end of IPL season with full accuracy stats.
